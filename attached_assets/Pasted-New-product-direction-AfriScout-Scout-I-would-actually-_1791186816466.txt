New product direction
AfriScout → Scout

I would actually consider removing the geographic limitation from the product name/positioning while preserving AfriScout as the brand if you already have the deployed product.

New positioning

Scout — The Global Opportunity Intelligence Network

Discover opportunities. Verify them. Match them. Build reputation. Act onchain.

The core idea becomes:

GLOBAL WEB
   ↓
Apify
   ↓
Opportunity Intelligence
   ↓

   ↓

   ↓
On-chain Identity & Reputation
   ↓
Verified Opportunities
   ↓
Applications / Contributions / Bounties
   ↓
Solana
   ↓
Payments / Rewards / Credentials / Reputation

This is much more interesting than simply adding a wallet-connect button.

5. Opportunity Verification

This is where blockchain can solve a genuine problem.

Currently AfriScout says:

"This opportunity came from this source."

The upgraded system can say:

This opportunity has a verifiable provenance record.

For example:

Opportunity
     ↓
Original source
     ↓
Apify collection
     ↓
AfriScout verification
     ↓
Content fingerprint
     ↓
Solana record

The chain does not need to store the entire opportunity.

Instead, store something like:

Opportunity ID
Content Hash
Source Hash
Timestamp
Verification Status

This creates an immutable reference to the version that was observed.

23. The new product loop

This is the part I would emphasize heavily in the hackathon presentation:

DISCOVER
   ↓
UNDERSTAND
   ↓
MATCH
   ↓
VERIFY
   ↓
PARTICIPATE
   ↓
COMPLETE
   ↓
GET VERIFIED
   ↓
BUILD REPUTATION
   ↓
UNLOCK BETTER OPPORTUNITIES

That creates a network effect.

The more people participate, the more verifiable activity exists.

The more organizations participate, the more opportunities exist.

The more verified activity exists, the better matching becomes.
